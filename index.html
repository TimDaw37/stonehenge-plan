A georeferenced plan of Stonehenge

Not GNSS-surveyed. OSGB from the Rees 1989 sheet (MP/STO0861). WGS84 is converted from that grid. Google Earth imagery of the same stones can sit about 1.5 m east of this plan; we do not warp the sheet to match GE. 91 and 93 sit on the surviving stones. Diamonds at 91, 92 and 94 are indicative excavated stoneholes from Cleal 1995 Plan 1.
