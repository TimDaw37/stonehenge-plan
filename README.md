# A georeferenced plan of Stonehenge

Live: https://timdaw37.github.io/stonehenge-plan/

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click for an OSGB coordinate. **show numbers** / **hide numbers** toggles the Atkinson/Cleal stone numbers. **show lidar** / **hide lidar** puts Environment Agency 1 m LiDAR hillshade under the stones.

The drawing is the 1990 measured survey (M. J. Reek / Rees for English Heritage), georeferenced from that sheet’s own OSGB ticks. It is not a GNSS ground survey. Other published OSGB plans (for example Cleal 1995) sit about two to five metres away on the same stones. The Heelstone sits 0.7 m from its EA LiDAR DSM peak. Use it as a click-plan, not as proof of a fraction of a degree.

LiDAR: Environment Agency 1 m composite, OSGB, © Environment Agency, Open Government Licence.
