# A georeferenced plan of Stonehenge

Live: https://timdaw37.github.io/stonehenge-plan/

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click to pin. Hover and pin both show OSGB and WGS84. **show numbers** / **hide numbers** toggles the Atkinson/Cleal stone numbers. **show lidar** / **hide lidar** puts Environment Agency 1 m LiDAR hillshade under the stones.

It is not a GNSS ground survey. Stone numbers sit on hatch centroids from the Rees 1989 sheet (Historic England MP/STO0861). OSGB is that sheet. WGS84 is converted from those grid coordinates. Google Earth imagery of the same stones can sit about 1.5 m east of this plan (checked at Station Stone 93); we do not warp the sheet to match GE. Residual vs Rees is a few tenths of a metre on the sarsens. The Heelstone is about 0.7 m from its EA LiDAR DSM peak. Use it as a click-plan, not as proof of a fraction of a degree.

LiDAR: Environment Agency 1 m composite, OSGB, (c) Environment Agency, Open Government Licence.
